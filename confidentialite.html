Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Mindshort publishing tool accesses, why it accesses it, how long it is kept, and what choices you have. The tool is operated by Maxime Mandeville, doing business as Mindshort, in Longueuil, Quebec, Canada (referred to below as "Mindshort", "we" or "us").

The Mindshort tool is a content management and publishing tool. An authorized user signs in with TikTok, chooses a video, writes its caption, and publishes the video to that user's TikTok profile.

1. Information we access through TikTok

When you sign in with TikTok and authorize the tool, TikTok shares information with us, limited to the permissions you grant. We access only the following:

  • Basic account information (the user.info.basic permission): your TikTok open ID for the application, your display name and your avatar image. We use this to identify the account that signed in.
  • Profile information (the user.info.profile permission): your username, display name, avatar, bio and public profile statistics, such as follower and following counts. We display this inside the tool so you can confirm the connected account before posting.
  • Videos and captions you choose (the video.upload and video.publish permissions): when you select a video and ask the tool to publish it, the tool receives the video file and the caption you wrote, so it can upload them to TikTok and post them to your profile.

We do not receive your TikTok password. Sign in is handled entirely by TikTok. We do not access your private messages, your contacts, your drafts on other platforms, or any information outside the permissions listed above.

2. How we use this information

  • To show you which TikTok account is connected, so you can confirm you are posting to the right profile.
  • To upload and publish the videos you select, with the captions you write, to your TikTok profile.
  • To keep the tool working and secure, and to respond to support or privacy requests you send us.

We use the information only to operate the tool, for the purposes described here.

3. What we never do

  • We never sell personal information, and we never rent or trade it.
  • We never use TikTok data for advertising, marketing profiles or audience building.
  • We never publish anything to your TikTok account without your action and your authorization.
  • We never request permissions beyond the ones the tool needs in order to function.

4. How information is shared

We share information only in these limited cases:

  • With TikTok, so that the video and caption you chose can be uploaded and posted to your profile, and so the connected account can be displayed back to you.
  • When required by law, for example in response to a valid legal request or court order.

Other than that, we do not disclose personal information to third parties.

5. Data retention

We keep personal information only for as long as it is needed to operate the tool and to meet legal obligations. Access tokens issued by TikTok are kept only while the connection they support is in use. Profile information is used to identify and display your connected account; it is not compiled into a separate marketing database. When you revoke the tool's access, or when you ask us to delete your information, we delete the stored information associated with your account, except where the law requires us to keep it.

6. Revoking access

You stay in control of the connection. You can revoke the tool's access at any time from your TikTok settings, in the section of the TikTok app or website where connected applications and app permissions are managed. Once access is revoked, the tool can no longer view your profile information and can no longer publish anything to your account. You can also write to us at any time to ask what information we hold about you or to request its deletion.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect the information the tool handles, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) when information moves between the tool and TikTok. No system is perfectly secure, but access to the tool and its data is limited to the operator.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the privacy legislation that applies in Quebec and in Canada, you may have the right to access your personal information, to ask for it to be corrected, to request its deletion, and to withdraw your consent to its use. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at maxime.mandeville1@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time and no later than the deadline set by applicable law.

9. Children

The tool is a business publishing tool intended for account holders who manage their own TikTok profiles. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the tool.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version will always be posted on this page, with its "Last updated" date at the top. If a change is significant, we will take reasonable steps to point it out on this page.
