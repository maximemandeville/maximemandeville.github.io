The app

Mindshort App

The publishing tool for Mindshort's TikTok accounts, operated by Maxime Mandeville in Longueuil, Quebec, Canada. This is the working screen of the tool: connect a TikTok account, confirm it, then publish finished Mindshort videos to that profile.

Connect your TikTok account

The app connects to TikTok through TikTok Login Kit. Click the button below to open TikTok's own sign in page, review the permissions requested, and authorize the connection. TikTok handles the sign in itself: the app never sees or stores your TikTok password.

After you authorize, TikTok returns you to this website and the app confirms which account is connected, showing its display name, username and avatar before anything can be published.

Sign in with TikTok

You can review or revoke the app's access at any time from your TikTok settings. Revoking the access disconnects the account from the app.

Publish a video

Once an account is connected, the publishing screen of the app works in five steps. The user stays in control at every step, and nothing is posted without the user's own publish action.

1

Choose the video file

Select a finished video file from your own library. The app does not browse, download or reuse anyone else's content.

2

Write the title and caption

Enter the title and the caption exactly as they should appear with the video on TikTok.

3

Set the privacy setting

Choose who can view the post on TikTok before sending it, using the privacy options TikTok offers for the connected account.

4

Publish

Press the publish button. The app uploads the video to TikTok through the Content Posting API and posts it to the connected profile.

5

Check the status

The app shows the publishing status returned by TikTok, so the user can confirm the post went through and view it in TikTok.

Publishing requires a connected TikTok account. If no account is connected, the publish action stays unavailable and the app asks the user to sign in first, using the button in the section above.

Permissions used

The app only requests the TikTok permissions it needs to connect an account and publish content the user has chosen. Here is what each permission is used for, in plain language.

  • Basic account information (user.info.basic) Identifies the account that signed in, so the app knows which profile is connected.
  • Profile information (user.info.profile) Shows the display name, avatar and public profile details inside the app, so the user can confirm the account before posting.
  • Video upload (video.upload) Sends the video file the user selected to TikTok, ready to be posted.
  • Video publishing (video.publish) Publishes that uploaded video, with the user's caption, to the user's own TikTok profile.

Full details about the information the app accesses and how it is handled are in the Privacy Policy, and the rules for using the app are in the Terms of Use.
